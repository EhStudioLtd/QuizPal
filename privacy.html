Privacy Policy

Introduction

Quiz Pal ("the App") is a trivia and general knowledge game operated by Eh Studio Ltd ("we", "us" or "our"), a company registered in the United Kingdom.

Eh Studio Ltd is the data controller for personal information processed under its responsibility in connection with Quiz Pal.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and other applicable privacy legislation.

Quiz Pal is a general-audience trivia game with family-friendly content and may be used by children and teenagers as well as adults. The App does not ask a player's age or date of birth and does not verify age. A store age rating is a content rating, not a way for us to identify an individual player's age. We apply relevant protections required by laws that apply to our service.

Quiz Pal does not require users to create an account, provide their real name or supply an email address to play.

This Privacy Policy explains how gameplay information is stored, what subscription-related information is processed by third-party services and how users can exercise their privacy rights.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Gameplay Information

Quiz Pal allows players to enter a name or nickname to identify themselves during gameplay. This does not need to be their real name.

The following information is stored locally on your device:

  • Player names or nicknames.
  • Game preferences and settings.
  • Quiz scores and statistics.
  • Recent question history and game records.

This gameplay information is not transmitted by the App to Eh Studio Ltd or RevenueCat.

We do not maintain an external database containing player names, quiz answers or gameplay scores.

We cannot remotely access or retrieve information stored only on your device.

No Quiz Pal account registration or email address is required to play.

We encourage all players to use nicknames instead of real names.

1.2 Subscription Information

Quiz Pal uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service, to display subscription offerings, verify purchases and manage access to premium features.

After a user continues past the welcome screen, the App configures the RevenueCat SDK. On later launches it is configured as the App starts. In either case, configuration happens before the user chooses whether to start a subscription or free trial; it is not limited to occasions when a user opens a separate subscription screen.

Quiz Pal does not provide RevenueCat with a player name, email address or custom user ID. When the SDK is first configured, RevenueCat generates an anonymous App User ID to identify this app customer and their subscription status. The ID is pseudonymous: it does not state the user's name, but it can be linked to purchase and subscription records and may still be personal information. It may be created before any purchase.

RevenueCat may process:

Depending on SDK and store activity, RevenueCat processes the App User ID, purchase history and transaction information, subscription status and entitlements, and related subscription events. Locale and currency information may also be available. RevenueCat's current Google Play guidance says its SDK does not collect approximate or precise location; it identifies advertising/device identifiers as conditional on integrations that use them. Quiz Pal does not call RevenueCat's device-identifier collection method.

RevenueCat uses the App User ID to find the customer record in its dashboard and associate subscription status and purchase history with that customer. This is a pseudonymous identifier, not an identity check, and it does not tell us the user's age or real-world identity.

We may access relevant subscription records through the RevenueCat dashboard for subscription management and support purposes.

Quiz Pal does not intentionally transmit player names, quiz answers or gameplay scores to RevenueCat.

1.3 App Update Information

Expo Updates checks for and downloads software updates. Its requests can include a random identifier for this App installation, platform and app-update details, the device's IP address, and, after an update failure, update identifiers or launch-error text. Expo uses this information to deliver and manage updates and measure update usage. See section 5.4 for details.

1.4 Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we may receive:

  • Your email address.
  • Your message and any attachments.
  • Information you voluntarily provide about a technical issue or subscription.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, resolve problems and handle privacy requests.

Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information, particularly information relating to anyone under 18.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We process limited personal information for purposes associated with operating Quiz Pal.

The purposes and lawful bases below describe our intended processing under the UK GDPR. We must assess each activity before relying on a basis; listing a basis here does not itself establish that its legal conditions are met.

Performance of a Contract

Where a person enters into a subscription, we rely on performance of that contract to process purchase and entitlement information necessary to provide the subscription or an eligible free trial, restore purchases, and resolve related issues. This basis applies only to processing necessary for that contract. It does not automatically cover information about a child using the same device, pre-purchase identifiers, or unrelated processing.

Legitimate Interests

For support enquiries, our legitimate interest is responding to the person and resolving their concern. For proportionate security and fraud-prevention activity, our legitimate interest is protecting the App, purchases and users. We will only rely on this basis where processing is necessary and our interests are not overridden by the individual's rights and interests, taking particular care where users under 18 may be affected. Support correspondence is retained as described in section 7.3.

We configure RevenueCat after a user continues past the welcome screen and at app launch on later visits, before a user chooses a subscription or trial. This means a pseudonymous App User ID and related subscription-service information may be processed before a purchase, including for users whose age we do not know. Our purpose is to check and manage subscription access and support the paywall and purchase flow. We rely on legitimate interests for necessary pre-purchase processing only where our interests are not overridden by the user's rights and interests, with particular consideration for children. We will keep this assessment under review. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests using the contact details below.

For Expo Updates, our interests are delivering software updates and maintaining the security, reliability and performance of the App. Expo also uses update request information to measure update adoption and reliability. We rely on legitimate interests for these purposes only where the processing is necessary and our interests are not overridden by users' rights and interests, with particular care where children may be affected.

Legal Obligations

We process information under the legal-obligation basis only where a specific applicable law requires us to do so, such as responding to statutory data-protection requests or retaining particular accounting or tax records. This does not mean that all subscription information or support emails must be retained for accounting purposes.

Consent

On first use, the welcome screen presents links to these Terms and this Privacy Policy before the user continues. By continuing, the user indicates agreement to the Terms and acknowledgement of this Privacy Policy. The App stores a flag that the welcome screen has been completed, not a separate record of the policy version accepted. This step does not ask or verify age, establish parental authority, or constitute consent to personal-data processing where consent is legally required. We do not rely on consent as the lawful basis for RevenueCat processing. If we introduce an activity that legally requires consent, we will obtain valid consent before that activity begins and provide a way to withdraw it.

We do not require a separate Quiz Pal account or personal contact details for gameplay. Access requires the relevant subscription entitlement, which can include an eligible free trial. Apple or Google may require an app-store account to start a subscription or trial.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Children and Younger Users

Quiz Pal has no minimum-age check or age-verification step. We do not ask players for their age or date of birth. The App is general-audience and family-friendly, so children may use it; the age ratings and audience selections made in app stores do not tell us the age of an individual player.

Because children may use a family-friendly app, we consider children's interests when designing the App and deciding how personal information is processed. The App does not ask for age or date of birth, so we cannot identify which players are children. We apply the safeguards described below to all players and do not treat the store's age rating or welcome-screen acceptance as proof of a player's age or parental authorisation.

3.1 Gameplay Information

Player names or nicknames, game settings, scores, statistics, recent question history and game records are stored locally on the device. This gameplay information is not transmitted by the App to Eh Studio Ltd or RevenueCat. We encourage all players to use a nickname rather than a real name.

Quiz Pal does not provide public player profiles, direct messaging or social networking features.

3.2 Subscription Processing and Younger Users

On first use, RevenueCat is configured only after the user continues from the welcome screen, where the Terms and Privacy Policy are presented. On later visits, it is configured as the App starts. It is therefore configured before the paywall is opened and may create a pseudonymous App User ID before a user makes a purchase. The App does not check age or obtain parental consent before this processing, and the ID does not reveal a user's age. We do not intentionally send player names, quiz answers or gameplay scores to RevenueCat. We do not use player names or scores for advertising or profiling.

Our safeguards include not requiring an account or real name, keeping gameplay data on the device, encouraging nicknames, and not using advertising networks or cross-app advertising tracking. A parent or guardian should help a younger player review subscription and free-trial terms before purchase. These measures reduce the information collected for gameplay; they do not make the App an age-verified service or amount to parental consent for RevenueCat processing.

A child or teenager should ask a parent or guardian to review these Terms and help with any subscription or free-trial purchase. The app-store purchase screen shows trial eligibility, duration, price, renewal and cancellation terms.

3.3 Questions About a Younger User's Information

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us or through a service used by Quiz Pal, contact us at support@happy-life.biz. Gameplay information remains on the device and is not accessible to us. We will assess the report and take steps required by applicable law.

4. Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

Quiz Pal does not display third-party advertisements or use advertising networks.

We do not track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

We do not operate a separate gameplay analytics service that collects player names, quiz answers or gameplay scores. Expo Updates measures update usage and reliability as described in section 5.4; RevenueCat provides subscription reporting as described above.

RevenueCat provides subscription reporting and may process purchase events, subscription status and paywall interactions.

Such processing supports subscription functionality, service reporting and related operations.

Subscription-related technical information may be processed even when no purchase is made.

4.1 Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

Quiz Pal does not use personal information for behavioural profiling, targeted advertising or automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on users.

Subscription access is determined automatically using purchase and entitlement information processed through RevenueCat and the relevant app store.

This automated verification is used to determine whether premium features are available.

4.2 No Sale of Personal Information

Eh Studio Ltd does not sell users' personal information or share it with third parties for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Personal information may be disclosed to service providers where necessary to operate subscription functionality, provide support or comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use personal information collected through Quiz Pal for targeted marketing to children.

7. Data Storage and Retention

7.1 Gameplay Information

Gameplay information is stored locally on the user's device.

We do not maintain an external gameplay database.

Quiz Pal does not separately encrypt its local gameplay storage.

Device or cloud backups may include locally stored App information depending on operating system settings.

Local information generally remains until removed through available App controls, device storage management or deletion of the App.

Removing a player from the player list does not necessarily delete all associated historical game records.

Backups may retain copies after locally stored data is deleted.

7.2 Subscription Information

Subscription records are used to establish access, restore purchases and handle subscription support. RevenueCat’s DPA provides that customer personal data is processed for the term of the agreement and describes deletion or return at the end of service, subject to its terms and legal requirements. We can request deletion of a customer record using RevenueCat’s customer-deletion tools where applicable. Apple and Google separately control their own transaction records under their policies and legal obligations.

RevenueCat and store records may remain after cancellation or deletion of the App; neither action automatically deletes all associated records. We retain or ask RevenueCat to retain information only as needed for subscription access, restoration, support, security and legal requirements, and will assess deletion requests. The 12-month support-email period does not apply to subscription records. The precise deletion timing may depend on RevenueCat’s agreement, system processes and legal requirements.

Deleting Quiz Pal does not cancel an active subscription.

7.3 Support Correspondence

We retain support correspondence for up to 12 months after an enquiry is resolved, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary for a legal obligation or dispute resolution. We apply this period to our mailbox records, including sent copies, and periodically delete or anonymise correspondence when it expires.

IONOS may retain routine system backups or copies for periods set by the email service. These may not be immediately removed when we delete a mailbox message and are governed by the applicable IONOS service and backup cycle.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information processed in connection with Quiz Pal.

Gameplay information remains locally stored rather than being transmitted to an external gameplay database operated by us.

Subscription-related information is processed through RevenueCat and the applicable app store. Expo also processes app-installation and update information as described in section 5.4.

We encourage users to:

  • Protect their devices with appropriate security settings.
  • Keep their operating systems updated.
  • Avoid entering sensitive information as player names.
  • Review their device and cloud backup settings.

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.
