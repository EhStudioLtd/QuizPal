Privacy Policy

Introduction

Quiz Pal ("the App") is a trivia and general knowledge game operated by Eh Studio Ltd ("we", "us" or "our"), a company registered in the United Kingdom.

Eh Studio Ltd is the data controller for personal information processed under its responsibility in connection with Quiz Pal.

We are committed to protecting user privacy, including children's privacy, in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and other applicable privacy legislation.

Quiz Pal is designed for individual and family entertainment and may be used by children, teenagers and adults.

Quiz Pal does not require users to create an account, provide their real name or supply an email address to play.

This Privacy Policy explains how gameplay information is stored, what subscription-related information is processed by third-party services and how users can exercise their privacy rights.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Gameplay Information

Quiz Pal allows players to enter a name or nickname to identify themselves during gameplay. This does not need to be their real name.

The following information is stored locally on your device:

  • Player names or nicknames.
  • Game preferences and settings.
  • Quiz scores and statistics.
  • Recent question history and game records.

This gameplay information is not transmitted by the App to Eh Studio Ltd or RevenueCat.

We do not maintain an external database containing player names, quiz answers or gameplay scores.

We cannot remotely access or retrieve information stored only on your device.

No Quiz Pal account registration or email address is required to play.

We encourage users, particularly children, to use nicknames instead of real names.

1.2 Subscription Information

Quiz Pal uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service, to display subscription offerings, verify purchases and manage access to premium features.

In the current version of the App, the RevenueCat SDK is configured during app launch, before a user chooses whether to start a subscription or free trial. It is not currently limited to occasions when a user opens a separate subscription screen.

When the RevenueCat SDK is configured without a custom user identifier, RevenueCat automatically generates a random anonymous App User ID.

This identifier may be generated before a subscription is purchased.

RevenueCat may process:

  • An anonymous App User ID.
  • Device platform and operating system information.
  • App version and related technical information.
  • Country, language and locale information.
  • Subscription status and premium access entitlements.
  • Purchase and transaction records, where applicable.
  • Subscription-related activity timestamps.
  • Information associated with paywall presentation and subscription interactions.

RevenueCat may use IP addresses to determine approximate country information.

Although an anonymous App User ID does not directly identify a person by name, it may still constitute personal data under applicable privacy laws.

We may access relevant subscription records through the RevenueCat dashboard for subscription management and support purposes.

Quiz Pal does not intentionally transmit player names, quiz answers or gameplay scores to RevenueCat.

1.3 Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we may receive:

  • Your email address.
  • Your message and any attachments.
  • Information you voluntarily provide about a technical issue or subscription.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, resolve problems and handle privacy requests.

Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information, particularly information relating to children.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We process limited personal information for purposes associated with operating Quiz Pal.

The purposes and lawful bases below describe our intended processing under the UK GDPR. We must assess each activity before relying on a basis; listing a basis here does not itself establish that its legal conditions are met.

Performance of a Contract

Where a person enters into a subscription, we rely on performance of that contract to process purchase and entitlement information necessary to provide the subscription or an eligible free trial, restore purchases, and resolve related issues. This basis applies only to processing necessary for that contract. It does not automatically cover information about a child using the same device, pre-purchase identifiers, or unrelated processing.

Legitimate Interests

For support enquiries, our legitimate interest is responding to the person and resolving their concern. For proportionate security and fraud-prevention activity, our legitimate interest is protecting the App, purchases and users. We will only rely on this basis where processing is necessary and our interests are not overridden by the individual's rights and interests, taking particular care where children may be affected. Support correspondence is retained as described in section 7.3.

For subscription access, we currently configure RevenueCat at app launch, before a user chooses a subscription or trial. This means pre-purchase identifiers and related technical data may be processed. We rely on legitimate interests for this limited pre-purchase processing only where it is necessary and our interests are not overridden by the user's rights and interests, with particular consideration of children. We will review and document this assessment and change the app flow if this basis is not appropriate. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests using the contact details below.

Legal Obligations

We process information under the legal-obligation basis only where a specific applicable law requires us to do so, such as responding to statutory data-protection requests or retaining particular accounting or tax records. This does not mean that all subscription information or support emails must be retained for accounting purposes.

Consent

We do not currently rely on consent as the lawful basis for RevenueCat processing at app launch. Reading this policy, using the App, or approving an app-store purchase does not itself constitute consent to personal-data processing. If we introduce a processing activity that legally requires consent, we will obtain valid consent before that activity begins and provide a way to withdraw it.

We do not require a separate Quiz Pal account or personal contact details for gameplay. Access requires the relevant subscription entitlement, which can include an eligible free trial. Apple or Google may require an app-store account to start a subscription or trial.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

3. Children's Privacy

Quiz Pal is a trivia game intended for a broad audience, including families and children under the age of 13.

We recognise that children's personal information requires particular protection.

3.1 Children's Gameplay Information

Children can use Quiz Pal without creating an account or providing their real name, email address, telephone number or home address.

Players may enter a nickname.

Player names, quiz answers and gameplay scores remain stored locally on the device.

This information is not transmitted by the App to Eh Studio Ltd or RevenueCat.

Quiz Pal does not provide public player profiles, direct messaging or social networking features.

3.2 Privacy by Default

Quiz Pal is designed to minimise the personal information required for gameplay.

Our approach includes:

  • No mandatory account registration.
  • No requirement to provide a real name.
  • Local storage of gameplay information.
  • No public player profiles.
  • No direct messaging between players.
  • No advertising networks.
  • No intentional use of gameplay information for behavioural profiling.
  • No intentional sharing of gameplay information with external analytics services.

RevenueCat is configured at app launch, before the user opens the paywall. The App does not ask for age or use a verified parental-consent flow before this occurs. We apply privacy protections to all users and are completing a child-focused assessment of this startup processing, including necessity, balancing, and safeguards. If the assessment does not support this processing for children, we will change the flow or SDK configuration before continuing it in that context.

3.3 Subscription-Related Information

RevenueCat may generate an anonymous App User ID and process limited technical information when its SDK is initialised, including when Quiz Pal is used by a child.

This processing supports subscription management, purchase verification and premium feature access.

RevenueCat may also process subscription-related events and paywall interactions.

We do not intentionally send children's player names, quiz answers or gameplay scores to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat is not used by us to deliver behavioural advertising.

At launch, RevenueCat may create or use a random anonymous App User ID and communicate with RevenueCat before a player chooses a subscription or trial. The App does not ask the player's age before this happens, and store purchase approval does not control this earlier activity. An identifier that does not contain a name may still be personal information.

3.4 Purchases and Parental Supervision

The free trial is configured for eligible subscription products in Apple App Store Connect and Google Play Console. Trial availability and eligibility may differ by store account. The store purchase screen shows the trial duration, price after the trial, and renewal and cancellation terms. A trial is time-limited subscription access, not a permanently free gameplay tier, and may renew as a paid subscription unless cancelled under the terms shown.

Purchases are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on the user's device.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise younger players and use the purchase approval and parental control features provided by Apple or Google.

App-store authentication and purchase approval do not necessarily constitute parental consent for personal data processing.

Parents or guardians should manage trial enrolment and subscription purchases. Approving payment is separate from authorising the processing of a child's personal information. If consent is required for a processing activity, that activity needs an appropriate consent mechanism before it can proceed.

3.5 Parents' Rights Regarding Children's Information

Quiz Pal does not require player registration and does not collect or store player names, quiz answers or gameplay scores on our servers. This information remains locally on the user's device and is not accessible to Eh Studio Ltd.

RevenueCat, our subscription management provider, processes anonymous App User IDs and related technical or subscription information. These records are not directly associated with a player's name or a Quiz Pal account, but an anonymous identifier may still be personal data.

Parents and legal guardians may contact us at support@happy-life.biz with questions or requests relating to their child's personal information.

Where a relevant RevenueCat App User ID or transaction identifier is available, we may be able to locate the associated subscription records and assist with applicable access or deletion requests.

If we cannot reasonably identify records associated with a particular individual, we will explain this limitation and respond in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

We do not require children or parents to provide additional personal information solely for gameplay.

3.6 Information for Younger Players

If you are a child using Quiz Pal:

You can use a nickname instead of your real name.

Your quiz name and scores are saved on your device.

We do not receive your quiz answers or scores.

A subscription service uses a special code to recognise the App installation and check whether premium features are available.

Playing requires subscription access. An eligible free trial may be available, but it can become a paid subscription. Ask a parent or trusted adult to check the price, trial period and cancellation terms before starting.

Please ask a parent or trusted adult before making a purchase or contacting us.

4. Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

Quiz Pal does not display third-party advertisements or use advertising networks.

We do not track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

We do not operate separate gameplay analytics services that collect player names, quiz answers or gameplay scores.

RevenueCat provides subscription reporting and may process purchase events, subscription status and paywall interactions.

Such processing supports subscription functionality, service reporting and related operations.

Subscription-related technical information may be processed even when no purchase is made.

4.1 Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

Quiz Pal does not use personal information for behavioural profiling, targeted advertising or automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on users.

Subscription access is determined automatically using purchase and entitlement information processed through RevenueCat and the relevant app store.

This automated verification is used to determine whether premium features are available.

4.2 No Sale of Personal Information

Eh Studio Ltd does not sell users' personal information or share it with third parties for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Personal information may be disclosed to service providers where necessary to operate subscription functionality, provide support or comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use personal information collected through Quiz Pal for targeted marketing to children.

7. Data Storage and Retention

7.1 Gameplay Information

Gameplay information is stored locally on the user's device.

We do not maintain an external gameplay database.

Quiz Pal does not separately encrypt its local gameplay storage.

Device or cloud backups may include locally stored App information depending on operating system settings.

Local information generally remains until removed through available App controls, device storage management or deletion of the App.

Removing a player from the player list does not necessarily delete all associated historical game records.

Backups may retain copies after locally stored data is deleted.

7.2 Subscription Information

Subscription records are used to establish access, restore purchases and handle subscription support. RevenueCat's DPA provides that customer personal data is processed for the term of the agreement and describes deletion or return at the end of service, subject to its terms and legal requirements. We can request deletion of a customer record using RevenueCat's customer-deletion tools where applicable. Apple and Google separately control their own transaction records under their policies and legal obligations.

RevenueCat and store records may remain after cancellation or deletion of the App; neither action automatically deletes all associated records. We retain or ask RevenueCat to retain information only as needed for subscription access, restoration, support, security and legal requirements, and will assess deletion requests. The 12-month support-email period does not apply to subscription records. The precise deletion timing may depend on RevenueCat's agreement, system processes and legal requirements.

Deleting Quiz Pal does not cancel an active subscription.

7.3 Support Correspondence

We retain support correspondence for up to 12 months after an enquiry is resolved, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary for a legal obligation or dispute resolution. We apply this period to our mailbox records, including sent copies, and periodically delete or anonymise correspondence when it expires.

IONOS may retain routine system backups or copies for periods set by the email service. These may not be immediately removed when we delete a mailbox message and are governed by the applicable IONOS service and backup cycle.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information processed in connection with Quiz Pal.

Gameplay information remains locally stored rather than being transmitted to an external gameplay database operated by us.

Subscription-related information is processed through RevenueCat and the applicable app store.

We encourage users to:

  • Protect their devices with appropriate security settings.
  • Keep their operating systems updated.
  • Avoid entering sensitive information as player names.
  • Review their device and cloud backup settings.

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.
