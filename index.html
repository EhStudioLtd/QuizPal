About the App

Quiz Pal is a fun, multiplayer quiz game where up to 8 players compete by answering trivia questions across various categories and difficulty levels. It’s perfect for game nights, classroom fun, or friendly competitions.

Features include:

  • Customizable Rounds – Choose how many questions each player answers per game.
  • Difficulty Levels – Select from Baby, Child, Teenager, Adult, or God mode to match your challenge preference.
  • Timed Questions – Set a custom time limit per question to keep the game exciting.
  • Score Tracking – Points are automatically tracked. The player with the most correct answers wins!

Quiz Pal is designed for all ages. Younger players may need guidance depending on the difficulty level selected.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Quiz Pal free to use?

No, Quiz Pal requires a subscription to access its full features. We offer monthly and annual plans, both with a 7-day free trial so you can try the app before committing.

2. Can I play Quiz Pal offline?

Yes! Once the app is downloaded and activated with a subscription, Quiz Pal can be played without an internet connection. Perfect for travel or offline game nights.

3. I'm experiencing a bug. What should I do?

If you run into a technical issue, please email us with a description of the problem, your device type, and app version. Our support team will get back to you promptly.

4. How does scoring work?

Players earn 10 points for each correct answer. There are no penalties for incorrect answers. The player with the highest total score at the end of the game wins!

5. How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

All subscriptions are managed through Apple. You can cancel or manage your subscription at any time by going to your iOS device’s Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
